# Decentralized Credit Scoring

## Project Description

The Decentralized Credit Scoring project is a blockchain-based smart contract system that revolutionizes traditional credit scoring by leveraging on-chain financial behavior data. Unlike conventional credit scoring systems that rely on centralized authorities and limited data sources, this decentralized solution creates transparent, immutable, and globally accessible credit profiles based on actual blockchain transactions and loan repayment history.

The system automatically calculates and updates credit scores in real-time based on users' financial activities, including loan repayment patterns, transaction volumes, and payment timeliness. This creates a more inclusive financial ecosystem where users can build creditworthiness through their on-chain activities, regardless of their traditional banking history.

## Project Vision

Our vision is to democratize access to credit assessment by creating a transparent, fair, and globally accessible credit scoring system that:

- **Eliminates Geographic Barriers**: Provides universal access to credit scoring regardless of location or traditional banking infrastructure
- **Promotes Financial Inclusion**: Enables unbanked and underbanked populations to build credit history through blockchain activities  
- **Ensures Transparency**: Makes credit scoring algorithms and data publicly verifiable on the blockchain
- **Reduces Bias**: Uses objective, algorithm-based scoring that eliminates human bias and discrimination
- **Empowers Users**: Gives individuals complete control and ownership of their credit data
- **Facilitates Global Commerce**: Creates interoperable credit scores that work across borders and platforms

## Key Features

### 🔐 **Decentralized Credit Profiles**
- Immutable credit records stored on blockchain
- Real-time credit score updates based on on-chain activities
- Transparent scoring algorithm visible to all participants
- Credit scores range from 300-850, following industry standards

### 💰 **Comprehensive Loan Management**
- Authorized lender system for institutional credibility  
- Detailed loan recording with amounts, due dates, and terms
- Automatic payment tracking and history maintenance
- On-time payment rewards and default penalties

### 📊 **Advanced Credit Scoring Algorithm**
- **Payment History (35% weight)**: Rewards consistent on-time payments
- **Default Ratio (20% weight)**: Penalizes missed payments and defaults
- **Transaction Volume (15% weight)**: Considers total financial activity
- **Transaction Frequency (10% weight)**: Values regular financial engagement
- **Additional factors (20%)**: Account age, loan diversity, and other metrics

### 🛡️ **Security and Governance**
- Owner-controlled contract with authorized lender management
- Role-based access control for sensitive operations  
- Event logging for complete audit trails
- Ownership transfer capabilities for governance evolution

### 📈 **Real-time Updates**
- Automatic credit score recalculation after each transaction
- Event-driven architecture for efficient monitoring
- Historical data preservation for trend analysis
- Timestamp tracking for all activities
